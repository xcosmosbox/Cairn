# ADR 0005: Annotation revision history and v0.1 fidelity contract

Status: current v0.1 product contract adopted through [#107](https://github.com/xcosmosbox/Cairn/issues/107)
on 2026-10-26; implementation remains pending in its finite child tasks. #105 earlier
superseded the historical revision method and shipped single-pass behavior. The failed #63 calibration remains failed: 11 of12 originals materialized.
The later typed-critique revision implementation was software evidence, not improved coverage.

At the PR106 baseline, the production path makes one original-source annotation attempt, validates actual
source/alias/schema properties, and keeps valid independent material. It does not perform
semantic-idempotence Gate B, losslessness rewrite, feedback repair or repeated annotation
rollbacks. An omitted description or failed document is counted locally, without erasing
successful siblings. Original UUID-form literals are source text; machine identities still
use request-local aliases and stable owned UUIDs. Human-curated edits remain protected.

Earlier method details and test receipts remain in git history and #64/PR72. They cannot be
relabelled as results of this simplified method. New evidence records the actual source and
raw outcomes. Run affected annotation/incremental checks; fullrace, full609, counter receipts,
archival reconstruction and repeated live GO are not prerequisites for a small experiment.

No schema, immutable Bundle or query-side write/LLM behavior is introduced. Rollback reverts
the implementation commits while preserving historical inputs/results and human data.

## Current v0.1 applicability

The [v0.1 plan](../coordination/reports/2026-10-26-v01-build-completeness-plan.md)
supersedes single-pass/no-semantic-revision and omission-only-success as product goals.
Finite configured retries target a failed step; malformed assessment retries verification
of the same candidate, while source-semantic defects revise the offending document with
originals/candidate/feedback and recheck the whole candidate. Successful siblings remain;
exhaustion is explicit partial/unavailable, cancellation/permanent errors have reasons.
Meaningful omissions or distortions remain unaccepted; empty output is not proof that a
nonempty original contains no knowledge. HTTP retries do not replace semantic revision.
This documents the new intended behavior, not its completion. #63/#64/#105 results and
the removal of peripheral archive/cost/time/full609/repeated-GO gates remain unchanged.

## Source subjects and their direct predicates

The fixed public mini repeat exposed a subject-binding gap: annotation retained a dated
duration in a separate attribute Concept, and a dated quantity in separate version/date
Entities, while the original named subjects retained only definitions or duties. Full
mapping preserved those member divisions; description fusion received only each node's
declared members. All source incidences and numeric strings survived globally, but the
subjects lost their required facts or source-bound reachable paths. This is not semantic
completion, and later relation-audit uncertainty does not explain away the earlier gap.

Generation and the existing semantic assessment now share an explicit boundary: when the
original directly states attributes, states, relations or rules about an explicitly named
subject, retain those facts and their complete arguments and qualifications in that
subject item's content/detail, whether dated or undated. Additional attribute/date or
statement/rule units may coexist but cannot replace that subject's material or leave its
assertion only in another relation participant. Preserve roles, direction, speakers,
quoted stance and negation rather than reversing the predicate or promoting a disputed
claim to an author-confirmed fact.
This does not require copying a sentence's predicate indiscriminately to every mentioned
object or generating an inverse relation that the source did not assert.
A temporal qualifier alone does not establish a new subject identity. Preserve genuinely
distinct objects and explicitly distinct version objects, different subjects, dated values,
negation, conditions and exceptions. Source uncertainty is distinct from assessment unknown:
an explicitly unknown value or unspecified subject may be faithfully retained and verified
as supported. Only an assessor unable to determine whether the candidate's attribution or
uncertainty is faithful returns unknown. Never guess an owner to create a connection the
source did not specify. Neither similar names, dates, values nor entity/concept style
authorizes a programmatic merge.

The existing document processor supplies the original, whole candidate and concrete
ownership omission to finite revision, then reassesses the whole replacement. There is
no new schema, model call, retry loop, allowance or invented graph relation. This helper
is shared by Full and C4. Its assessment is also reused for single-node fusion; that
caller checks only the node's supplied member material and attribution, without inventing
an absent parent, changing members or importing unsupplied facts. Whole-document items
assessment remains responsible for the annotation subject boundary. At that annotation-only
repair boundary, mapping and alignment were unchanged; the clarification could not guarantee
that a model would choose a connected final representation.

Scripted controls establish omission-feedback flow, finite repair/exhaustion, preservation
of independent identities and dated/negative facts, and same-candidate unknown handling.
They already pass on the unchanged processor and do not prove prompt effectiveness. The
actual failed repeat remains failed; validation after the change must use the same fixed
sources and unchanged subject/source/reachable-value acceptance. Prior successful Full
and changed results, earlier failures and all planned cases remain historical evidence.

A subsequent fixed repeat completed production with all three relation scopes complete;
the ticket/count values and source bindings were retained, while acceptance still failed:
an undated dependency existed only in a standalone statement unit and the target ticket's
material. The named entry subject had only its SKILL member, without the log source. This
showed that date/version qualifiers must not be a prerequisite for direct-subject material.
The same generation/assessment boundary now covers undated predicates; no proposition is
mechanically merged into an action and no edge is forced. Both failed repeats and their
different outcomes remain unchanged. New scripts exercise this exact omission shape and
wrong-subject/quoted-claim feedback through the existing finite processor, not model
effectiveness or a guarantee of later mapping membership.

## Mapping source subject identity

The following fixed repeat retained the entry's log source, but acceptance was still
5 PASS / 1 FAIL / 1 UNKNOWN. Mapping received all14 accepted items and grouped the generic
counting subject's duties separately from its dated counting fact. The latter's qualified
name and different tag were presentation choices, not proof of an independently identified
object. All member IDs being structurally covered did not establish correct subject identity.

The existing mapping invocation now defines equivalence consistently in its system and
assembled user instructions as identity of the referenced subject/concept, established
from complete name/content/detail and source context. Complementary observations and
different dated/conditional facts about the same subject may share members without being
whole-sentence synonyms. Preserve each member/source, value, date, condition, negation,
speaker and disagreement for complete later description; do not average or erase differences.
Name normalization, dates, version wording, tags, common predicates or identical detail
alone do not authorize merging. Truly independent objects/version objects, distinct contexts,
unresolved identities, standalone propositions/rules and their mentioned participants remain
separate references when their meanings differ. In particular a sentence can describe both
a counting actor and a counted object without making those objects identical.

Only prompt instructions change. There is no mapper semantic assessor or new global judge;
the current coverage helper checks missing member/source incidence structurally. ID/UUID,
exact deduplication, coverage algorithms, retry allowances, call topology and schema remain
unchanged, and no business edge is manufactured. The existing annotation controls remain.
Focused scripted replies establish complete input context, parsing of grouped/separate
members, structural coverage and lossless source/member material flow. They cannot establish
that a model will choose the intended grouping. Actual after validation remains necessary
on the same fixed inputs and acceptance criteria; the failed repeat and earlier outcomes
are retained without promotion.

## Node predicate and negation scope

The next fixed repeat passed its finite artifact/query checks and negative controls, yet
independent semantic review found a later meaning loss. Mapping retained both subject
members. An earlier nominal relative clause had a faithful reading, and the full member
Detail explicitly excluded participation in the whole admission activity. Node generation
changed that to excluding only admission's counting matters; its assessment accepted the
narrower scope. This does not establish an actual participation event, but it fails to
retain the original broad exclusion. Automatic PASS receipts and this semantic FAIL remain
separate preserved evidence; the active changed run used the original source unchanged.

Node generation and its existing single-node assessment now share one predicate-scope
instruction. Moving from a nominal modifier to a full sentence must preserve subject,
arguments, negation and modifier scope; a category head describing the subject cannot
become a newly restricted negation object. Conversely, a source's narrow subclass exclusion
cannot become a blanket exclusion. Each assertion in summary and detail must be faithful;
a correct field or a correct statement elsewhere cannot cancel another field's error.
An explicit complete member sentence may be retained or separated into unambiguous clauses,
but this is not a universal Detail-wins priority: genuine source differences remain
attributed and unresolved where appropriate. Faithfully preserved source uncertainty may
be supported; an assessor unable to verify fidelity still returns unknown.

Only the two existing node prompt roles share this rule. The finite per-node flow,
successful-sibling retention, IDs, graph schema, allowances and call topology are unchanged.
Scripts exercise concrete forward/inverse scope feedback, repair/exhaustion, exact-candidate
unknown handling, independent siblings and faithful uncertainty/source differences. They
can pass the parent prompts and prove flow rather than model detection or repaired live
semantics. The actual affected cases still require their own same-input after validation.

## Incremental version identity and material scope

In the changed phase of that repeat, alignment assigned an incoming bare month-version
unit from a ticket context to an old counting-version node. The complete batch identified
the incoming owner, and the candidate summary/description identified the counting owner;
the shared month did not establish one object identity. Later source-separated paragraphs
inside a single UUID could not repair that unsupported identity assignment. The phase
was conservatively blocked before writeback and remains failed. Failure to recall the
correct old ticket-version target did not authorize merging into a different candidate.

The existing C4 alignment prompt now applies referent identity consistently to merge_into
and batch fuse. Complementary facts need not be whole-sentence synonyms to belong to one
proven object. Complete supplied name/content/detail, candidate summary/description, source
and batch owning-subject context establish identity; a bare version name, date, value or
tag alone does not. Versions of different owners and independently identified version
objects are not forced together. Same-owner/same-version cross-source material, or an
explicitly shared version object/global version identifier, can establish one identity;
different source files alone do not establish different objects. Mentioned participants,
standalone propositions and their subjects remain distinct referents. Unproven identities
use the existing attach/new_subdomain choices without guessing owners or aliases.

Only the existing alignment prompt changes: exact-alias validation, legal merge UUID
pinning, members/sources, FTS recall, candidate limits, schema, finite flow and allowances
remain unchanged. An unretrieved matching old target can still result in a new duplicate;
this change neither supplies missing candidates nor invents identity to avoid duplication.
Four SQLite-backed scripted cases exercise actual recall, judgment parsing, aliases,
materialization, stored-old-object preservation and all source incidences for same-owner
versions, a shared global release, different owners and an unproven owner. Their replies
are supplied by the test; parent prompts can pass them too. They establish material flow,
not model identity recognition or live efficacy. Actual after validation is still required.

A separate node-generation/assessment clarification distinguishes material omission from
whole-source omission and reality absence. Missing provided-member information does not
establish that the complete source omitted a fact, that a fact does not exist, or that
an assertion has unlimited applicability or no constraints. Do not invent blanket absence
or unknown statements from that omission. Preserve source-explicit unknown, unspecified
or uncertain facts with their actual subject, time, scope and attribution. A necessary
local observation must identify the provided member and its limited scope; neither
generation nor assessment may promote it to a whole-source claim. Consider all supplied
contributions without mechanically preferring Content or Detail. The existing assessment
still returns unknown when fidelity cannot be established. This does not assert that the
old member-scoped wording was necessarily false or that January values contradicted the
whole source. The clarification uses the same two node prompt roles and existing finite
flow; its scripted repair/exhaustion/unknown checks also pass the parent and prove flow
only. Neither clarification relabels the previous automatic or independent semantic results.

## Subject ownership as annotation's organizing contract

The later fixed actual08 repeat completed production with 6 PASS / 1 FAIL across its
seven checks. The entry subject still lacked the log source. Earliest log generation
emitted a complete dependency proposition and its other participant, but no item referring
to the directly predicated activity; the following assessment accepted it. The recorded
generation and assessment system bytes matched the deployed subject rules. This establishes
a generation/assessment miss despite those rules, not lost feedback, an exhausted allowance
or a mapper mistake. Separation of an independent proposition from an activity is legitimate;
the missing subject-bearing contribution is the defect. Whether ordinary action names were
interpreted as ineligible subjects is only a hypothesis about model behavior.

Generation now starts by organizing source-addressable subjects/concepts and their facts,
with optional independent statements afterward. Its former trailing direct-subject paragraph
and assessment's corresponding late paragraph are replaced by one shared early ownership
contract. Ordinary actions, processes, methods and roles can be subjects without proper
names or independent definitions; either entity or concept can carry their facts. A whole
proposition containing a subject's name is not automatically an item referring to that
subject. Whole-document assessment treats source-to-direct-subject carrying, combined-item
substantive coverage and each field's faithfulness as separately necessary. Missing required
carrying uses existing concrete omissions with the subject, original assertion and qualifiers;
it does not introduce a new response field, parser, judge, retry loop or allowance.

The shared core preserves actual argument roles, direction, quotation, speaker stance,
negation, conditionality, versions and faithful source uncertainty. A proposition merely
being discussed does not assert its content; uncertain or generic attribution does not
authorize guessing a particular owner. Neither mentioning an object nor sharing a name
requires copying all facts to it, forced merging or an inverse edge. Assessment states
the single-node exception before the shared core: the real node caller's supplied members
and current node define its scope, with no new parent, changed membership or graph regrouping.
This does not exempt whole-document annotation from its source-subject obligation.

Twelve scripted cases exercise actual DocumentProcessor repair/exhaustion, retention of
all four existing items, complete-candidate recheck, ordinary/proper subjects, discussion,
uncertain/generic ownership, quoted denial and source-explicit unknown. The single-node
case calls the real FuseNodeDescriptions and shared SemanticGate through the existing
caller scope. Supplied verdicts establish data/feedback/finite-flow boundaries only; they
can also pass parent prompts and do not demonstrate model detection or efficacy. Actual08
and all earlier outcomes remain unchanged. After exact publication the runtime owner will
separately assess the recorded bad candidate, a controlled complete candidate and a faithful
single-node candidate, then decide the affected fixed-input mini from actual evidence.
No provider action occurs in this source change; no whole-mini or broader acceptance is
claimed by offline checks or by preparing those probes.

## Same-call subject-coverage result contract

The three actual leaf checks on the published subject-core change still accepted the
recorded missing-subject four-item candidate with a generic positive verdict; the complete
candidate and the correct single-node candidate passed too. This confirms another actual
whole-document false positive. Moving instructions earlier did not establish reliable
ownership checking. Those outcomes and the earlier 6 PASS / 1 FAIL mini remain unchanged.

Whole-document assessment now requires structured subject_coverage in the same existing
assessment response before an otherwise complete positive verdict can pass. Each row has
an exact source_quote, its subject_referent and only relevant candidate checks, each with
item_index, item_referent, explicit same_referent/preserves_assertion booleans and a short
result reason. A row has a carrier only when both booleans are true in a check. A complete
proposition may preserve the sentence but refer to a different object than its subject;
that is a lawful candidate, not automatically an unsupported item. A missing carrier adds
concrete existing omissions naming the subject, source assertion/qualifiers and candidate
comparisons. Other concrete rejection reasons and unsupported indices survive jointly;
revision retains valid statements and endpoints and rechecks the entire candidate within
the original shared finite allowance. No extra model stage, judge or retry loop is added.

The validator checks required typed fields, nonempty referents/reasons, literal quote
occurrence, in-range indices, duplicate JSON keys and duplicate indices within a row.
Different rows may share a quote or an item: one sentence can contain several subjects or
direct assertions. Quote context can include anaphora/conditions; preservation concerns
that row's subject assertion, not unrelated context. There is no name/date/keyword identity
matcher and no all-subject-by-all-item matrix. Empty rows require a concrete
no_direct_subjects_reason (for example, a title without a predicate); nonempty rows cannot
also claim that absence. These checks anchor declared results; they do not prove that
every real direct assertion was listed, that two referents are truly identical, or that
the booleans/no-subject explanation are semantically correct.

Malformed supplied mappings are invalid assessments even with a negative top-level verdict,
and consume only the existing same-candidate reassessment allowance. When no mapping was
supplied, an explicit rejection or existing nonempty omissions retain their known semantic
feedback rather than being washed into unknown. A generic legacy positive with empty
omissions cannot bypass the new evidence requirement. Unknown remains exactly status and
a concrete reason, without mixed verdict/evidence fields.

Scope is selected by trusted program construction: NewSemanticGate remains whole-document,
including one-item inputs; the sole node-fusion caller uses explicit NewNodeSemanticGate.
Neither model output, item count nor prompt-text inspection can choose the exemption.
The node gate retains its previous verdict contract and supplied-member/per-field fidelity
obligations. Shared fidelity instructions and the two scope-specific result formats are
composed separately so the whole prompt does not also offer the old four-field positive
as a complete response. Generation instructions are unchanged. This intentionally changes
the model-response contract, while persisted graph/database schema, original inputs, UUIDs,
human-edit protection, provenance, call topology, allowances and oracle remain unchanged.

Existing whole-positive scripts are migrated with source-specific authored quotes,
referents and carrier results, never a name-equality proof generator or legacy-pass adapter.
Several old transport controls packaged multi-subject prose into one shared-name row;
their corrected candidates add the actual carriers while preserving original retention,
source/UUID and finite-flow checks. The eight supplement originals and nineteen cases are
unchanged; their scripts now name their real library/channel/bracket, report, pump, valve,
controller/logger/sensor, cooling and battery referents. Duplicate-name fact retention and
literal UUID checks remain. A local-contradiction control had inherited unrelated prerequisite
annotation for enabled/disabled or refurbished-Mac sources. Its stub now carries the actual
activity and distinct statements; its original verifier evidence, condition checks,
compatible-advice rejection/revision, SQLite relation count and human-edge no-op preservation
remain exercised. Node-only/relationship verdicts and historical cached checkpoints are not
given fabricated whole-document evidence. Initial migration failures are retained as logs.

New deterministic controls exercise real default-whole rejection, trusted node acceptance,
no-carrier semantic revision/exhaustion, lawful candidate retention, malformed evidence
reassessment, jointly retained negative critique, empty-source cases and shared quote/index
rows. They establish response-contract and finite-flow behavior, not model detection or
efficacy. The later fixed bad leaf must accurately identify the missing real subject and
return concrete semantic feedback, either through valid coverage results or through the
contract's existing explicit-negative/omission form without mapping. Mere invalid-response
blocking does not demonstrate detection. A complete whole-document positive must include
its valid mapping, and the single-node check must remain correct before deciding
the affected fixed-input mini. No actual after result or broader acceptance is claimed here.
